## Goal

- (N21) Very hungry, no food around: the person goes looking farther instead
  of standing still.
- (N44) A band on a strip of land between two big waters does not loop
  "nothing to do" / "looking for water" until it dies.
- (E2) `go_home` never beats eating when hunger is 90–100 and food is underfoot.
- (E3) Nobody walks away from known water without considering the way back.

## Background

M15 close (changelog 2026-10-15/16, `docs/m15_hunger_resolution_20261016.md`):
the urgent food search no longer cancels before the first move; rest/sleep/
shelter lose to an available food action from hunger 70; exploration's hunger
interrupt stays at 98. Still open: search **without a nearby destination**.
K2 (phantom fresh shores at the map edge, fixed by 2.19) may explain N44.

## Steps

1. Capability tests (hand-built maps, no randomness):
   - `m16-hungry-searches-farther`: hunger > 70, no food in sight or memory →
     the person moves (net displacement > N tiles in M ticks), and a counter
     `explore_for_need` increments.
   - `m16-water-between-lakes`: band on a strip between two large waters with
     **no drinkable water reachable on foot** → not stuck alternating
     `idle`/`seek_water`: it moves, or a visible notice says there is no water.
     Variant: drinkable water reachable → they drink.
   - `m16-eat-before-home` (E2): hunger 95, food underfoot, home far → eats.
2. Run all on the build after 2.19. Passing ones are regressions (R22).
3. Failing N21/N44: add one behaviour "explore for a need" in a **new module**
   (`ai/NeedSearch.ts`, R14) hooked from `Brain`: when a need is high and has
   no candidate, pick an unexplored frontier cell from `PlaceMemory` (never
   read unknown world state), walk there, observe. If no frontier is reachable,
   emit a notice (R6) once per day.
4. E3: before scoring a far task, compare distance to the nearest **known**
   water with the remaining thirst margin; skip tasks that would strand them.

## Done when
