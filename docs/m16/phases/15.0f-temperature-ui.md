## Steps

1. Show the outdoor temperature and, when inside, the room's, in °C.
2. House panel: a sentence explaining body heat and insulation.
3. Do not reveal unknown occupants, names or temperatures of distant unseen
   houses: go through `Knowledge.ts` (R8).
4. Every text through `t()` with complete Spanish (R7).
5. Panels update by digest, not by rebuilding the DOM every frame (R10).
6. e2e with `?skipIntro=1`, no console errors, new dated screenshot
   `artifacts/screenshots/m16-<date>-15.0f/`; add the spec to `scripts.e2e`.

## Done when

e2e passes; the player can see why a room is less cold. The UI decimal never
feeds back into the physics.
