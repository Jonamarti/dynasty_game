## Goal

Owner: during a raid, going home must weigh, but not so much that raiders
abandon their companions. They check whether they are accompanied; if they see
companions going home or fleeing, they lose heart and go back.

## Files

`entities/Person.ts` (`raidingBandId`, `raidingUntil`), `ai/Drives.ts` (home
pressure), new `social/Rivalry.ts` (logic), hook in `Brain`/`Drives` (R14).

## Steps

1. Add `motivation.rivalry: false` with migration and test (R23).
2. While `raidingUntil` is in force, home pressure × 0.4 **if** the person sees
   at least one raid companion still going forward.
3. Morale: each think, the share of companions in sight that flee or head
   home; ≥ half → their raid ends and they go back.
4. No companion in sight → normal home pressure.
5. Counters `raid_held`, `raid_broke`.

## Tests

A party of four: nobody drifts home one by one; if two flee, the other two go back.

## Done when

Tests pass; R13 holds.
