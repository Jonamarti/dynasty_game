## Background (checked on 0.15.160)

Animals **already** flee (`fleeX`/`fleeY` in `WildlifeSystem`), predators
already bite (`core/AnimalAttack.ts`), and `Animal.temperament` exists (drawn
0–1 at birth, `Animal.ts` ~l. 243). This phase extends that mechanism; it does
not create another.

## Steps

0. Inventory in the diary: what triggers flight and attack today, per species.
1. Flight distance per species (`SpeciesDef`), modulated by `temperament`.
2. Predators, and prey with young, may attack a human who comes too close
   (chance and distance per species, R16 values in `SpeciesDef`).
3. Tamed animals do not flee their owner (nor members of the owner's band);
   animals being tamed flee less from the tamer.

## Tests

A deer flees at its set distance; a wolf with cubs attacks; a tamed dog does
not flee its owner.
