## Steps

1. **Proposal screen** (when the player proposes):
   - attendees with their stance;
   - the **reason** only for people the player knows well (ask `Knowledge.ts`
     for the acquaintance level; `known` and above). For the rest, only the
     stance (R8);
   - checkbox "Use authority" if the character is chief: recomputes with the
     same functions as 5.3 and 5.4 and shows who would accept and how much
     opinion it would cost;
   - hands count per path and a confirm button.
2. **"Band decisions" screen**: the player's band proposals **the player's
   character attended**: what, who, when, yes/no, coerced, state. This is where
   a chief being refused becomes visible (B11).
3. R6, R7, R10 (`[hidden] { display: none; }`, digest-based redraw).
