## Goal

Three effects respect the technology system like everything else: hunting uses
the weapon's reach; armour protects only as much as the wearer's knowledge of
it allows; refining hafting improves felling.

## Background

`techPower(person, techId)` (imported in `ActionSystem.ts` ~l. 78) scales an
effect by how well the person knows a tech. Many effects read it (e.g.
`arboriculture` ~l. 2107, `bone_working` ~l. 3469). `doHunt`, `armourOf`
(`knowledge/Tech.ts`) and `doChop` (~l. 2627) did not at 05-10.

## Steps

1. Three unit tests, one per effect: two people identical except for tech
   knowledge (or weapon reach) get different results.
2. If 0.2 found A9 already fixed for some of them, keep those tests and skip
   the change.
3. Apply `techPower` at the single place each effect is computed.

## Done when

Three tests pass; re-pinned.

## Watch out

`hunt` was calibrated against others ("`hunt` scored zero until its weight was
raised to nine"). Do not touch weights; run `ai-uses-many-actions` and report
the distribution before/after.
