## What the whole 15.0 series delivers (summary of the design)

- Outdoor temperature in °C per comarca: mean + annual cosine + daily curve
  (min 05:00, max 15:00) + small reproducible weather anomaly. Desert swings
  more by day; tundra is cold with a perceptible night drop.
- Each closed building with an interior has **one** room temperature that
  people warm (≈ +1 °C for one adult in a small hut, +2–3 °C for four,
  saturating below +4 °C), with thermal inertia (`tau` ≈ 0.08 days).
- Physical, not knowledge-gated: just being inside counts.
- Saved, travels with comarcas, shared by detailed/compact/parked simulation.
- The old scale (`TimeManager.temperature` ≈ −1…1) stays; one helper converts:
  `legacyUnits = (celsius − 10) / 20` (design parameters, not physiology).
- Out of scope here: per-tile air, wind, rain (that is 15.1), ventilation,
  new illnesses, new garments, graphical biomes.

## Steps (this phase)

1. Run the reference: `npm.cmd run sim:check` (band) and save it.
2. Find every use of `temperature`, `growth`, `dailyGrowth`,
   `thirstDriftPerTick`, `shelterAt` and fire heat
   (`grep -rn "\.temperature\b\|dailyGrowth\|thirstDriftPerTick\|shelterAt\|warmthFrom" src/sim`).
3. Classify each consumer: **outdoor**, **indoor**, **body rate**, **growth**.
4. Confirm where the climate profile/seed would come from in the constructor,
   the loader, the root world and parked comarcas.
5. Fix the parameters (legacy/new modes, conversion, policy for old saves:
   they stay in legacy mode).
6. Write the consumer table and the decisions in a new section "Inventory
   (15.0a)" at the end of `docs/m16_room_temp_plan.md`, **in English**.
