# M16 5.5 — Teach several at once (N1)

## Goal

Owner: gather those who can hear and want to listen, to teach a technology
to many at once instead of one by one.

## Steps

1. Proposal of kind `teaching` with a tech the proposer knows.
2. Extract from `teach` (ActionSystem) the function that computes what one
   pupil learns in a session. Apply it to each attendee who stays, meets the
   prerequisites and does not know the tech. **Do not write another formula.**
3. The session lasts as long as a lesson does today and is interruptible (R4);
   progress per pupil is banked as in today's lessons.

## Tests

Three pupils learn in one session what one learns today (each pupil's gain
equals the single-pupil gain).

## Done when

Test passes; R13 holds.
