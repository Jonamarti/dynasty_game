## Background

`ITEMS.herbs` (yarrow) exists and `tend` already spends one for a poisoning or
a festering wound (`Body.dress`, `Body.soothe`; comment in `Item.ts` ~l. 362).
M15 phase 17 animated "the healer bandages a wound" (changelog 2026-10-13) —
check whether a bandage item already exists before adding one.

## Steps

1. A `bandage` item (or poultice) with a recipe (e.g. plant fibre or hide
   strip) and an effect in the same pass.
2. Open wounds require herbs **and** a bandage; poisoning keeps requiring herbs.
3. Without them, `tend` refuses with a reason (R6); with them, the wounded
   improves and both are spent.
4. Measure `tend` counts before/after (C11: nobody tends the wounded already —
   do not make it worse; report).

## Tests

Refusal without materials, with its text; with them, improvement and spending.
