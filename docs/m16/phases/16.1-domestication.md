## Goal

- (N29) "Building herding" as the way to discover herding makes no sense: it
  comes from trying to tame a wild animal of that species.
- (N28) Dog taming cannot be learnt if there are no wolves on the map (same
  for cats and herd animals).

## Steps

1. New ingredient kind "tried to tame a <species>" (`saw`/`did` occasion,
   added to `NOTED_OCCASIONS` with words and Spanish), noted when someone
   attempts `tame` on a wild animal of that species.
2. Herding and per-species taming techs: replace the "built herding"
   ingredient with "tried to tame <species>".
3. If the species does not exist in the comarca, the tech is reached through
   contact with another band or comarca that knows it (existing teaching and
   news channels) — never blocked forever.
4. Test `every-gate-is-reachable` covers techs.

## Tests

Taming a wolf in a world with wolves enables dog taming; in a world without
wolves no unreachable tech is declared (reachable by contact).

## Done when

Tests pass; `techs-have-effects`, `spark-ingredients-are-real` pass.
