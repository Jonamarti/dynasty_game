## Goal

"I can't select NPCs on the coast." Anyone drawn on screen can be selected
with a click on their sprite, including someone wading or on the beach; a
click on open water still offers "drink".

## Background (checked on 0.15.160)

`main.ts` `candidatesAt` (~l. 1292): "A click on water is a click on water…"
— if `sim.world.isWater(Math.round(worldX), Math.round(worldY))` it returns
only `{ kind: 'ground' }`, **before** looking at people, animals or rafts.
Since M15 phase 27 people wade, swim and fish in water, so the premise no
longer holds. Also `Math.round` disagrees with `World.index`, which truncates
(`| 0`): with half a tile of offset, a click on someone on the beach often
lands on the neighbouring water tile (see "`Building` measures tiles from
their centres and `World` from their corners" in `bugs.md`).

## Steps

1. e2e `e2e/m16-select-on-shore.spec.ts` (`?skipIntro=1`; snap the camera
   first: `d.camera.snapTo(x, y); d.camera.following = false`): an NPC on a
   wading tile and another on the beach next to water are each selected by a
   click on their drawing; a click on open water still offers drinking. Add
   the spec to `scripts.e2e` (R26).
2. In `candidatesAt`: collect people, animals and rafts under the click
   **first**; the water short-circuit applies only if nobody is there.
3. Compute the click's tile the way `World` does. Add one shared helper (e.g.
   `tileOf(worldX, worldY)` in `src/sim/core/World.ts` or a small shared
   module) and use it in `candidatesAt` and wherever the renderer maps pixels
   to tiles. No `Math.round` copy.
