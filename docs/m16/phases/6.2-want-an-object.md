## Goal

People want objects for what they do, and go and get the materials — not only
craft what happens to be in their hands. Some also tidy loose materials into
nearby stores (N14).

## Steps

1. Extend `Lessons.remedy` (4.2) with three uses learnt by effect: `carry`
   (carries more), `work:<skill>` (works faster), `protect` (protects). Learnt
   when using a container, tool or garment, or seeing someone use one.
2. Purpose of kind `object`: born when the person knows the remedy, lacks the
   object and has agenda room. `priority = value × confidence × 0.5`.
3. Fulfilling it, in order:
   1. a loose one they may take (6.3);
   2. they know how to make it: fetch the ingredients — reuse the material
      search of sites (`gather_for_site` / the helper extracted in 2.15);
   3. it needs a station that does not exist: a site purpose.
4. An own item dropped to free the hands is remembered (where) and gives a
   purpose to recover it.
5. **Tidying (N14).** Owner: many materials lie on the ground instead of in
   the nearby store; it could depend on personality (orderly people) or on a
   mood malus from seeing mess in the tribe's area. Implement the first, small
   version: people with high conscientiousness/orderliness (use the existing
   temperament trait — no new trait, no new fork) score "carry loose band
   materials to a store with room" when idle-ish; counter `tidied_items`. Mood
   malus: leave for block 10 and note it in the diary.
6. **E23.** NPCs almost never placed furniture and could not get fat for lamps:
   make furniture and lamp fat object purposes through the same path and
   report counters.

## Tests

Someone who knows bundles and has none goes for sticks/grass and makes one;
`craft` makes more axes and spears than the baseline; an orderly NPC carries a
loose pile to a store.

## Done when

Tests pass; counters reported; R13 holds.
