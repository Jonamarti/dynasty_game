## Goal

Someone can call the band within earshot; those who want to listen come and
stand in front of them.

## Files

`ai/ActionCatalog.ts`; `ai/Brain.ts` (hook); `systems/ActionSystem.ts`
(`EARSHOT`, value 16); `social/Authority.ts`; new verbs through the usual
dispatch (label in `ACTION_LABELS`, test of 11.5 must pass).

## Steps

1. New action `convene`: the caller stands still; each adult or adolescent of
   their band within `EARSHOT` (16) gets `calledBy = { id, until }` for 60
   ticks. For the chief, reach 24.
2. New action `gather_round` for the called (do **not** reuse `attend`, which
   is for feasts). Score:
   `(0.2 + opinion/100 × 0.5 + bond + authority + curiosity × 0.2) × (1 − max need urgency)`.
   Never if any need is past the work line.
3. Attendees stand 2–3 tiles from the caller, facing them, in the rest pose
   (there is no sitting pose; do not touch the art).
4. **Already gathered**: 4+ adults of the band within 6 tiles count as present
   without calling.
5. When an NPC convenes: they have something to propose (an own site with
   fewer backers than needed, a teaching) and it is the first or last quarter
   of the day (`time.dayFraction`), or a gathering already exists.
6. Brake: a non-chief convenes at most once every 3 days; the chief once a day.
   Convening and getting no yes costs renown.

## Tests

With good opinion they come; with an urgent need they do not; a chief gathers
from farther.

## Done when

Tests pass; R13 holds; screenshot of a gathering.
