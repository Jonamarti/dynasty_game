## Goal

People learn "this species bears no fruit in season X" only when the bush is
really out of its fruiting phase — not because their band picked it bare.

## Background

`knowledge/SeasonLore.ts` has `barrenIn(kind, season)` (~l. 50) and
`barrenSeasons`. Observations come from `Simulation.observePlaces` (called in
`step`, ~l. 6790). A bush emptied in its own season is recorded as "no fruit"
and, seen two summers running, the species is learnt as barren in summer.

## Steps

1. Test: a bush picked to zero in summer, seen in two summers, is **not**
   learnt as barren in summer; a bush out of its fruiting phase is.
2. Where `observePlaces` records "no fruit", record it only if the bush is
   outside its fruiting phase (find the phase function: `grep -rn
   "bushPhase\|fruitingPhase\|inFruit" src/sim`). If no such function
   exists, R0.

## Done when

The test passes; re-pinned.

## Result (2026-10-20)

**Verified already fixed in M15 commit `19cd284`; no game code changed.**
`Simulation.observePlaces` records an empty berry bush as evidence of a barren
season only when `bushPhase` says the species is actually bare. An empty bush
in a ripening or holding season may have been harvested and is not evidence.
The existing capability test in `season-lore.test.ts` exercises two summers
after picking a bramble bush to zero and confirms summer is not learnt barren.
