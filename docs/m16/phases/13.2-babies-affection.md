## Goal

Owner: relationships toward babies of one's own tribe start a bit higher so
NPCs naturally care about them — e.g. 15 at birth, +1 each time someone plays
with the baby.

## Steps

1. Find the relationship module (`grep -rn "relationship\|opinion" src/sim/social/*.ts`)
   and where a newborn's relationships are created.
2. Every member of the baby's band starts at 15 toward the baby (R16), unless
   an existing rule already gives more (parents).
3. `play` with a baby: +1, capped (R16: cap 40 from play alone).
4. Counter `baby_play_bond`.

## Tests

After N plays the relation rises N; a baby of another band does not get the 15.

## Done when

Tests pass; counter reported; re-pinned (measure by counter, not survival).
