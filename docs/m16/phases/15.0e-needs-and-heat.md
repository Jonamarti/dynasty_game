## Steps

1. New mode and **one** adapter for cold and thirst rates from the local
   temperature. The UI reads the same result (no third formula).
2. No double counting: today `shelterAt` reduces exposure and adds recovery.
   In the new mode, separate environment reading from body insulation; keep
   legacy mode byte-identical.
3. Fire: a hearth/fireplace inside adds to its room's target; a person in
   that room does not also get the full radius bonus of the same fire. Outdoor
   fires keep proximity heat and do not heat a house through a wall. Torches
   keep their portable contract and do not heat the room. Garments reduce body
   exposure only when worn.
4. Thirst uses the same local reading; update Brain forecasts, compact
   ration requests and observers that call `thirstDriftPerTick`.
5. Snow reads outdoor, never a room. Growth keeps the old curves explicitly
   (regression test so it is not changed by accident).
6. Short scenario: no firemaking, no warm clothes, same shelter, same initial
   cold; occupancy 1 vs 4.

## Tests

The body of the group improves vs alone without knowledge or fuel (≈ 1–3 cold
points over a 0.25-day controlled night, ≥ 1 °C room difference — design
targets, not measurements); intense cold can still accumulate; legacy mode
fingerprint unchanged.

## Done when

Tests pass in both modes; counters reported.
