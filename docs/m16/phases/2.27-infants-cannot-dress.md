## Goal

A two-day-old baby is never ordered to put on armour or clothes, nor decides
to; an adult still is/does.

## Background (checked on 0.15.160)

`Person.age` is in **days**, but the gates compare with `1`:
`Brain.ts` ~l. 1157 (`person.age >= 1`, dressing when cold), ~l. 1176
(dressing hides), ~l. 1221 (removing for heat); `ToolEquipment.ts` ~l. 155
(`if (person.age < 1) return 'too_young_to_wear'`).

## Steps

1. Test `m16-infants-cannot-dress`: a 2-day-old cannot be ordered to dress
   (refusal `too_young_to_wear`, visible text) and the Brain never scores
   dressing for it; an adult can and does.
2. Find in `src/sim/entities/LifeStage.ts` (or wherever stages are defined)
   the age at which the "child" stage begins. Add one helper
   `canDressSelf(person)` there, and use it in the four places.
3. Never dress before that stage (D-18). A baby's warmth stays that of the
   carrier, as today.
