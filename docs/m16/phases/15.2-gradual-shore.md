## Goal

Owner: water does not turn into dry land in one step. Around rivers and seas
there are tiles — not always the same — of marsh, sand, mudflat or pebbles,
wet ground. From them you dig sand, pick up small stones, take water.

## Files

`world/WorldGeography.ts`, `world/LocalGeography.ts`, `core/World.ts`,
`entities/ResourceNode.ts`, `Item.ts` (only if a new item is needed).

## Steps

1. Shore kinds: `marsh`, `sand`, `mudflat`, `pebbles` — a per-tile attribute on
   shore tiles (not new biomes unless the renderer already maps biomes to art;
   decide and write why). Choice per tile from local context (river vs sea,
   slope, neighbours) plus `shoreRng` for variety.
2. Deposits: sand (dig), small stones (pick up), mud (the N32 tiles of 2.18
   come from here: `mudflat` and `marsh`), water (drink/fill). Each new
   resource needs a consumer in the same pass (no inert content); if `sand`
   has no consumer yet, do not add it — write it in the diary.
3. Placement in a pass **after `spawnPeople`** with `shoreRng` (`AGENTS.md`:
   never add to `spawnRng`). Append the fork after `foresightRng` and add the
   row to `AGENTS.md`.
4. Renderer: each kind is visible (art generator if sprites are needed; R9).

## Tests

No water tile touches dry land without at least one shore tile in between (on
maps; classic island: decide and test what holds there); mud tiles of 2.18
come from here; same seed same shores; `spawnRng` sequence unchanged.
