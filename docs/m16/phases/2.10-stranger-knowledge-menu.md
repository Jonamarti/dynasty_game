## Goal

The action menu on a stranger is the same whatever that stranger knows (R8).
`teach` and `discuss` are always offered; the **action** refuses, as `ask`
already does.

## Steps

1. Test: two strangers differing only in known techs produce the same menu
   for the player (same ids, same enabled flags).
2. In `ai/ActionCatalog.ts` `personActions`, remove any condition that reads
   the target's `knownTech` (or skills) unless the player knows them well
   enough through `social/Knowledge.ts`. Move the check into the action's
   refusal with a reason the player can see (R6).

## Done when

The test passes (or R22).

## Result — 2026-10-20

The capability test failed before the fix: `teach` and `discuss` had different
enabled flags for two strangers whose only difference was known technology.
`personActions` now consults `knowledgeOfPerson` before reading the target's
skills or technologies; while the target's character is unknown, the social
verbs stay available and their actions give the refusal. `KnowledgeSystem`
exposes the shared no-draw `canTeach` check; an explicit `teach` order with no
possible subject stops as `learned_nothing`. The discussion action already
stopped an uninformed partner as `partner_ignorant`.
