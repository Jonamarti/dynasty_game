## Goal

From the build menu the player can demolish a building of their band: it is
taken apart over time and returns part of its materials.

## Steps

1. Order `demolish` on a complete building of the player's band: a long
   action that wears `durability` down with the same arithmetic as `damage`
   (R4: interruption check; progress lives on the building).
2. At zero: the building is removed and leaves a pile with 50 % of its
   materials (R16) — through the same removal path as 14.8's ruins.
3. Refused with a reason (R6) if someone is inside, a site is in progress, or
   it is not the band's. Caves (`natural`) cannot be demolished.
4. Ruins (14.8) can also be demolished this way.

## Tests

Demolishing returns materials, frees the tiles, occupants leave first;
refusals have reasons; e2e from the build menu (add to `scripts.e2e`).
