## Goal

Each person holds beliefs about every band they know: how many, how strong,
how rich, how advanced — from what they saw, suffered or were told.

## Files

`social/Rivalry.ts`; `social/Knowledge.ts`; `social/Memory.ts`; news of M15
phase 36 (contact news).

## Steps

1. Per person and known band, four axes `{ value, confidence, day, source }`:

| Axis | Estimated from |
|---|---|
| people | distinct individuals of that band seen in the last 40 days |
| strength | armed people seen, raids suffered from that band, defences seen |
| wealth | their stores and herds seen, their feasts attended |
| technique | their objects and buildings seen whose tech is unknown to the viewer |

2. One's own band estimated the same way, from what the person knows of it.
3. `gap = (them − us) / (them + us)`, −1 to 1, per axis.
4. Told: in long conversations pass the highest-confidence figure at half
   confidence. Confidence −0.02 per day.
5. The band has no view of its own: the chief's counts.
6. 4.3's `coverage(safety)` now reads the strength gap.
7. Saved (R11).

## Tests

Whoever suffers a raid believes the rival stronger; whoever has seen nobody
holds no figure.

## Done when

Tests pass; R13 holds.
