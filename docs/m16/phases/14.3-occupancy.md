## Goal

Every shelter has a number of places. No more people sleep or shelter inside
it than that; the rest look for another shelter or propose one. This is the
base of the shelter ladder: small early shelters mean many shelters.

## Background (checked on 0.15.160)

- `BuildingDef` (`src/sim/entities/Building.ts` ~l. 26–187) has `shelter`,
  `interior`, `storage`, `sleepQuality`, but **no capacity field**.
- Sleep under a roof is resolved at a midnight sample in `Simulation`
  (~l. 4080–4190): `roofOverSleeper(person, buildingsById)` and
  `roofOverPrivateRest(...)` (from `systems/LifeSystem.ts`) decide each
  person's roof and fill `roofTonight` and a `byRoof` map. Conception requires
  both partners under the same roof (`LifeSystem.ts` ~l. 221–229).
- Shelter warmth: `NeedsSystem.shelterAt` (~l. 125): walled designs shelter
  their interior (`houseInteriorContains`), open ones a 1.5-tile margin.
- Night behaviour (M15 close, `motivation.nightExposure`) sends people home at
  night; `Brain` scores `sleep` under a roof by the company already there.

## Files

`entities/Building.ts` (new optional field), `systems/LifeSystem.ts`
(`roofOverSleeper`, `isUnderRoof`), `core/Simulation.ts` (midnight sample),
`systems/NeedsSystem.ts` (`shelterAt`), the `Brain` choice of where to sleep
(hook only, R14 — logic in a new `ai/Occupancy.ts`), `ui/` building panel.

## Steps

1. Add `places?: number` to `BuildingDef` with a comment: "how many people can
   sleep or shelter inside at once; undefined = not a sleeping shelter".
   Starting values (R16; tune in block 10):

| Design | places |
|---|---|
| windbreak | 3 (open; counts people within its margin) |
| lean_to (14.5) | 2 |
| mud_hut | 3 (after 14.6; today's value: 4) |
| wattle_hut | 4 |
| longhouse | 10 |
| cave (14.7) | from the cave's size |
| others with `shelter > 0` | write a value for each, from its interior area; list them in the diary |

2. New module `ai/Occupancy.ts`: `occupantsOf(building)` counted through the
   people spatial hash (R5) with `isUnderRoof`/the margin rule; and
   `hasRoomFor(building, person)`. Babies carried count with the carrier (0).
3. Midnight sample: a person whose roof is full (they arrived after the
   others — order by arrival tick, then id, never by array order) is **not**
   given that roof in `roofTonight` and gets a counter `roof_full`.
4. `shelterAt`: only the first `places` occupants get the building's shelter;
   the rest get the open-air value (or the margin of an open design).
5. Brain: the sleep/shelter target selection skips buildings without room
   (call `hasRoomFor` from the existing target filter). When every known roof
   is full, the person's band gets a reason to build: increment a counter
   `shelter_short` (4.x will turn this into a purpose; for now the planner's
   existing shelter branch sees more people than places — check that
   `planBuildings` counts **places**, not buildings; if it counts buildings,
   change it to count places).
6. UI: the building panel shows "Places: 2/3" for your band's buildings
   (through `Knowledge.ts` for others: R8). Text through `t()`.

## Tests

- Unit: a lean-to with 2 places and 3 people arriving at night: 2 get the
  roof, the third gets `roof_full` and open-air cold.
- Unit: arrival order decides, not array order (shuffle the people array:
  same result).
- `nights-are-slept` and `shelter-answers-cold` in `band`: report before/after
  (they may change; R18).
- Save/load round trip if any state was added (R11).

## Done when

Tests pass; re-pinned; counters `roof_full` and `shelter_short` visible in
the health report.

## Watch out

- Do not add a "max buildings" cap anywhere: the owner wants upkeep (14.8) to
  be the natural limit.
- Conception privacy (M15): a couple alone awake under a roof counts as roof.
  Do not break it — `roofOverPrivateRest` must also respect places.
