## Goal

Each person can say, once a day, how much of an upcoming season's need is
already covered, and how much they expect to suffer.

## Files

New `ai/Foresight.ts` (model: `ai/Drives.ts`); `entities/Person.ts`; inspector.

## Steps

1. `coverage(person, drive)` in 0–1:
   - `warmth`: **places** (14.3) in complete, non-ruined shelters usable by the
     person's band ÷ band members, capped at 1. (The old plan used roof area;
     with 14.3 places are the honest measure.)
   - `hunger`: food in stores the person may use ÷ (`weeklyFoodNeedPerPerson`
     × number of dependants × days of a season ÷ 7), capped at 1. Read
     `weeklyFoodNeedPerPerson` from where it is defined; do not hard-code.
   - `safety`: 0 for now (block 7 fills it).
2. `nearness(daysUntil, horizon) = clamp(1 − daysUntil / horizon, 0, 1)`.
   Horizon starts at 20 days for everyone; 4.7 makes it personal.
3. `expected(person, drive)` = max over future seasons within the horizon of
   `hardship.level × nearness × (1 − coverage) × foresight`, with
   `foresight = 1` until 4.7.
4. Computed once a day, stored on the person, **not** recomputed on load (R12).
5. Inspector: the three numbers for the directed character.

## Tests

- Full store → expected hunger 0.
- 25 days before winter → 0; 5 days before → high.
- Save/load keeps the stored value.
