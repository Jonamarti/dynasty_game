## Goal

Owner: "To build in an area you first have to remove trees, big stones,
crops, etc." A site is not ready until its footprint is clear.

## Files

`Simulation.placementRefusal` (today it checks terrain and other buildings
only); `entities/Building.ts` (model: how the silo stores its dig phase —
`earth`, `earthDone`, `dig`); the place in `Brain` that handles unfinished
digging (`grep -n "earthDone\|unDug" src/sim/ai/Brain.ts`).

## Steps

1. On placing a site, compute what stands on its tiles: trees and resource
   nodes (spatial hashes, R5). Store as `site.clearing: { kind, id }[]`
   (saved, R11).
2. A "clearing" phase before construction, like the silo's "dig":
   `addWork` refuses while `clearing` is not empty.
3. Trees go with `chop` (needs an axe after 14.4 — so a site on trees needs an
   axe; the cursor says so); the wood goes to the site pile and counts as
   delivered if the site needs wood. Stones go with whatever gathering verb
   they already have.
4. A sown field is not cleared: placement is refused with its reason.
5. The build cursor shows how many trees and stones must go.
6. NPC site choice (the extracted site finder of 4.4) prefers clear ground.

## Tests

A site over two trees does not progress until they are felled; the wood lands
in the site pile; placement on a sown field is refused with a reason.

## Done when

Tests pass; screenshot of the cursor; re-pinned.
