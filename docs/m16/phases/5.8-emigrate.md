## Goal

Owner: even if the tribe does not want to emigrate, the player can (a)
convince the tribe → everyone leaves; (b) convince a few (another dialogue
option) → they leave; (c) convince nobody → leave alone from the world-map
menu. In every case a menu chooses what to take from what they own (what they
can carry, plus animals and transport).

## Steps

1. Three routes: a proposal of kind `emigration` accepted by majority (all
   go); the same with "those who agree" (only the yes go); and a world-map
   menu entry "Emigrate alone" for the directed character.
2. Load menu before leaving: reuse `ui/TransferPanel.ts`. The limit is what
   the leavers can carry, plus animals and sledges/boats (reuse the travel
   capacity of M15 phase 35).
3. What does not fit stays and remains the origin band's.
4. A group that leaves without the whole band forms a **new band**: reuse the
   path that creates the band of the exiled today to claim the id.
5. Travel uses the existing comarca travel (`WorldState`, journeys).

## Tests

Each route moves exactly the people it should; the load respects capacity;
the new band exists with the leavers; save/load mid-journey works (E7 rules).
e2e of the menu (add to `scripts.e2e`).
