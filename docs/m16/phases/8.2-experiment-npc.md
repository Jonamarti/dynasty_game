# M16 8.2 — NPCs who experiment

| | |
|---|---|
| Stage / step | 10 / 107 |
| Switch | `experiment` (and the agenda needs `foresight`) |
| Changes the world | only with the switch on |
| Depends on | 8.1, 4.4 |

## Steps

1. Purpose kind `explore`: born if a drive has `expected ≥ 0.25` and **no
   known remedy**, or curiosity is high and immediate needs are covered. One
   per person.
2. What they do: pick with `experimentRng` an item kind or a place they
   **know** and have not experimented with in 10 days; go, take it, run
   `experiment`. **Never read the route table** (NPCs are not oracles).
3. Conceiving an idea this way gives renown.
4. Counters: experiments, ideas by this path.

## Tests

An NPC with an uncovered drive and no remedy experiments with known things
only; ideas per person-year stay under 3.

## Watch out

`ideas-are-conceived` has a ceiling of 3 ideas per person per year. Do not
raise it.
