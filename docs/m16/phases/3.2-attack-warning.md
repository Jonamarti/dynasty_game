## Goal

When the directed character is attacked, a red border shows how dangerous it
is; a blow shakes the screen slightly. A setting turns pulses and shaking off.

## Files

`social/Vulnerability.ts` (`fightingPower`, `vulnerabilityOf`),
`social/Defence.ts` (`assailantOf`), `WildlifeSystem`/`SpeciesDef` (animal
damage), `ui/Settings.ts`, `src/style.css`, `main.ts`.

## Steps

1. Sim exposes for the player `threatNow` in 0–1: human assailant →
   `vulnerabilityOf(player, assailant)`; animal → map its damage per bite to
   the same scale (find how bite damage is defined; if there is no measure, R0).
2. Intensity = `1 − (1 − threat) × (health / 100)` (0 with no threat and full
   health; rises with threat or with lower health).
3. UI: a red-border layer whose opacity is the intensity, pulsing at **at
   most one cycle per second**; on each blow received, shift the canvas a few
   pixels proportional to intensity. Never more than three flashes per second.
4. Setting "Reduce flashes and shaking" (D-11a) in `ui/Settings.ts`: no pulse,
   no shake, steady border. Saved with the other settings.
5. The attack does not interrupt an action in "You direct" (3.1).

## Tests

Unit of the formula with four cases (rabbit & health 100; bear & health 100;
human & health 100; human & health 20). e2e with a screenshot (add to `scripts.e2e`).

## Done when

Tests pass; screenshots with and without the setting.

## Watch out

R10 for the layer; no DOM rebuild every frame (update a style property).
