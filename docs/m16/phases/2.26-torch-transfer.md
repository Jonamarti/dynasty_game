## Goal

Automatic social transfers (amends, demands) move items through one transfer
contract that carries metadata such as torch fuel; a lit torch given in
amends stays lit or goes out with a reason; no slot is ever left without an owner.

## Files

`src/sim/social/Amends.ts`, the demand/exigency code, `docs/m15_phase12d_torches.md`
(torch fuel), `Simulation.handOver` (the transfer the player uses).

## Steps

1. Test: a lit torch handed over as amends keeps its fuel (or goes out with a
   reason); inventory slots stay consistent on both sides.
2. Make the automatic routes call the same transfer function the player route
   uses (`handOver` or the function it delegates to). Remove direct
   `Inventory` withdrawals there.

## Done when

Test passes; `grep` shows no direct inventory removal left in those routes.
