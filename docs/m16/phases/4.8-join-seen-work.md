## Steps

1. In the daily agenda pass, each person looks at unfinished sites of their
   band within sight (spatial hash, R5).
2. If the site is a remedy they know for a drive with `expected ≥ 0.12` and
   they have agenda room, they join as in 4.4.
3. If they do not know the remedy and have high curiosity, they go and look:
   check whether helping on a site requires knowing its tech. If not, helping
   already teaches (working alongside teaches faster, O3) — let them join. If
   it does, R0.
