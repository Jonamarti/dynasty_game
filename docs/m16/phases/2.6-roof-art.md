## Goal

"The building image with roof and without roof do not match: the walls are not
in the same place; the roofed view looks horizontal, not oblique." Both images
share anchor and oblique projection, so lifting the roof only removes the roof.

## Files

Building generators under `art/src/` (find them from `art/README.md`),
`docs/m15_art_contract.md` (projection contract), `docs/m15_art_pipeline.md`,
`src/render/Renderer.ts` (`drawBuildingSprite` with layers `'ext'` / `'plan'`,
~l. 1816–1823), `e2e/roof-lift.spec.ts`.

## Steps

1. Capture the mismatch: screenshot of one house with roof and lifted, same
   camera (extend `roof-lift.spec.ts` or a new spec).
2. Read the art contract; find which of the two generators (`ext` or `plan`)
   breaks the oblique projection or the anchor. Fix the generator only.
3. `npm run art:build`, `npm run art:sheet`, look at the sheet.
4. `src/render/__tests__/art.test.ts` must pass (sheets and generators agree).

## Done when

Walls coincide in both captures (saved under a dated directory); art test passes.

## Watch out

Never edit `public/art/` by hand (R9). Commit the regenerated PNG/JSON with
the generator change.
