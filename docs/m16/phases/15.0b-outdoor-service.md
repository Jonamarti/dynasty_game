## Goal

`temperatureOutsideAt(context, tick)`: same arguments, same number, no side
effects, no shared RNG.

New `src/sim/core/Climate.ts` (names indicative; if the checkout already has
helpers, reuse them).

## Steps

1. `ClimateProfile = { meanCelsius, annualAmplitudeCelsius,
   dailyAmplitudeCelsius, weatherAmplitudeCelsius, annualPhaseLag }`.
   Amplitudes are half-ranges (daily 7 = 14 °C min-to-max).
2. Starting profiles (R16, game values, not climate data):

| Profile | Mean | Annual | Daily | Weather ± |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Temperate | 12 | 12 | 4 | 2 |
| Hot desert | 24 | 8 | 10 | 2 |
| Cold desert | 7 | 15 | 9 | 2 |
| Tundra | −5 | 12 | 5 | 2 |
| Humid tropical | 25 | 2 | 2 | 1 |

3. Year phase is continuous: `(dayInYear + dayFraction) / daysPerYear` from
   the instance's calendar (never hard-code 40). Spring starts at phase 0,
   summer solstice 0.25, winter solstice 0.75; lag configurable, default 0.
   Legacy mode keeps the old curve's extremes (0.375 / 0.875).
4. Daily curve: two linked half-cosines, min 05:00, max 15:00 (10 h warming,
   14 h cooling), value and slope continuous at the joints and at midnight.
5. Earth maps: invert the annual phase by hemisphere; reduce the annual
   amplitude near the equator. Map Köppen classes of the current atlas
   explicitly (check their ids; do not guess). Random maps: one adapter from
   biome/regional climate. Classic island: Temperate. Unknown → documented
   fallback, never NaN.
6. Weather anomaly: points every 2 game days, each from
   `new RNG(derivedSeed(rootSeed, comarcaId, pointIndex, modelVersion))`,
   smoothly interpolated; include the absolute year. **No fork, no
   `Math.random`, no draw from a getter.** Cache profile and the two current
   points; caches are disposable, not authority.

## Tests (`climate.test.ts`)

Continuity across day and year boundaries; extremes at the configured
phases; desert daily amplitude > temperate; tundra mean lower; hemispheres
opposite; weather bounded, continuous, reproducible, different across years;
**thousands of queries in any order leave every RNG and the checkpoint
unchanged**.
