## Goal

(a) Furniture and stations are not drawn with the tribe's banner or band
ring. (b) Indoor furniture only inside a finished building of the tribe
(already true); outdoor furniture only inside the tribe's territory.

## Background (checked on 0.15.160)

- `Renderer.drawBuildingArt` (~l. 1777) draws the band-colour ring on every
  building with art; `drawBuildingSprite` (~l. 1823) draws `banner/pole` and
  `banner/cloth` (~l. 1844–1845) without looking at `def.furniture` or
  `def.station`. Bed, bedding, bench, shelf, rug, lamp, fireplace and the
  stations all get them.
- Indoor rule already exists: `Simulation.furniturePlacementRefusal` requires
  the own, complete household and being inside.
- There is no "outdoor furniture" class: stations (`station: true`, not
  `furniture`) are placed like buildings and never consult `territoryOwnerAt`.

## Steps

1. Tests:
   - `m16-furniture-unbannered` (renderer test with a fake context, like those
     in `src/render/__tests__/`): no `banner/*` asset and no band ring for a
     building whose def has `furniture` or `station`; yes for a hut.
   - `m16-outdoor-furniture-territory`: a station placed where
     `territoryOwnerAt(x, y)` is not the band is refused with a reason; inside,
     accepted; a band with no territory may place within 8 tiles of one of its
     finished buildings (D-16).
2. (a) In both renderer functions, skip ring and banner when
   `def.furniture || def.station`.
3. (b) In `Simulation.placementRefusal`, for `def.station && !def.furniture`:
   refuse unless `territoryOwnerAt(x, y) === band.id`, or the band has no
   territory and a finished building of the band is within 8 tiles (spatial
   hash, R5). Reason text through `t()` (R6, R7). The same predicate must be
   used by NPC site selection, so NPCs never try a refused spot.
4. Screenshot of a furnished interior and of a station outside.

## Done when

Both tests pass; screenshot saved; re-pinned if (b) changed any world.
