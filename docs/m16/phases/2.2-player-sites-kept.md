## Goal

The owner asked "do my planned buildings cancel themselves, or do other NPCs
cancel them?". Expected: a site the player marked out stays until the player
cancels it; an NPC site retired by the band planner leaves a visible reason.

## Background (checked on 0.15.160 — probably already done)

Fixed during the M15 close (changelog 2026-10-15, "el planificador ya no
retira las obras del jugador (N9)" and "conservar las obras del jugador…"):

- `BandSystem.dropStaleSites` (~l. 1987) skips `site.complete ||
  site.playerPlaced`.
- `Simulation.abandonSite` (~l. 5832) writes the reason to the sponsor's
  chronicle and pushes an `insights` entry at the site position.
- Test: `orders.test.ts`, "the planner never shelves a site the player marked
  out"; e2e: `e2e/site-abandonment-notice.spec.ts`.

## Steps

1. Write the capability test `src/sim/__tests__/m16-player-sites-not-dropped.test.ts`:
   (a) a site placed by the player, untouched for 10 days, still exists;
   (b) an NPC site untouched for `STALE_SITE_DAYS` + 1 days is removed and its
   sponsor's chronicle and `sim.insights` contain the reason; (c) materials
   delivered to (b) are left in a pile on the ground (count before == after).
2. Run it. If it passes: R22 — record "verified already fixed" and stop.
3. If a part fails, fix only that part in `dropStaleSites`/`abandonSite`.

## Done when

The test passes and README §7 says `verified already fixed <commit>` or `done`.

## Watch out

A site sponsored by an NPC that the player *ordered someone to work on* is not
`playerPlaced`. That is intended; do not widen the exemption.
