## Files

`ai/ActionCatalog.ts`, `systems/KnowledgeSystem.ts` (where an idea is
conceived), `knowledge/Synthesis.ts` (`sparkFires`, `sparkStatus`, `Notice`).

## Steps

1. Append `experimentRng` after the last fork; row in `AGENTS.md` (R2).
2. Action `experiment`: 60 ticks, interruptible (R4), label in `ACTION_LABELS`.
3. On completion, build the same `Notice` the daily spark draw uses and walk
   the techs whose prerequisites the person meets:
   - a route fully met → conceive the idea with probability 0.8 (draw from
     `experimentRng`);
   - none, but some route at least half met → a **vague hint**: the *kind* of
     the missing ingredient ("something to carry in your hands", "a place",
     "a season") — never which one;
   - otherwise nothing.
4. Limits: one hint per tech per day. One attempt in three spends one unit of
   what is carried in the hands.

## Tests

With a full route, 8 of 10 attempts conceive (fixed seed); 20 attempts in a
row never reveal an ingredient's name.

## Done when

Tests pass; screenshot of a hint.
