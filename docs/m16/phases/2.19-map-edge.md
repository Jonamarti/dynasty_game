## Goal

- (N34) Walking to the edge of the map shows no "the water was too deep to swim".
- (K1) The perimeter of a map world is not shore: no mud from digging there,
  no well or trap "touching water" there.
- (K2) No phantom fresh-water shores on the east edge caused by water on the
  west edge one row below.
- (N36) After scouting east and coming back, the tribe and family trees still
  show the living, and going back west does not say "the neighbouring comarca
  is under the sea" when it is land.

## Background (checked on 0.15.160)

- `World.biomeAt` (~l. 1034): out of bounds returns `'water'`. So `isWater` is
  true outside; `depthAt` 0 → `isShallow` **true** outside (K8); `isWalkable`
  false → `MovementSystem.nudge` returns `too_deep` (N34).
- `World.isShore` (~l. 1086) uses `isWater` of the four neighbours → every
  walkable perimeter tile is shore (K1) and enters `shoreTiles`/`shoreHash`.
  Consumers: `Earth.isWetSubsoil` (mud), `Simulation.touchesShore`,
  `earthworkRefusal`, rafts, swimming, hiding a body in water.
- `World.hasWaterOfKind` (~l. 714): `ownKind` checks `isWater` (true outside)
  and then indexes `waterKind[index(tx, ty)]`, and `index` wraps x past the
  width onto the next row (K2, E1).
- On the **classic island the edge really is sea**, and the order of
  `shoreTiles` is visible state (resources are spawned by walking it). A global
  change to `biomeAt` would move every classic seed.
- N36 hypothesis (not reproduced): "under the sea" comes from
  `destinationIsLand` on the neighbour of `WorldState.currentIdentity`, which
  reads `initialGeographicStart`; several reinstall branches of `WorldState`
  (~l. 369, 453, 518, 743 at 13-10) may restore the previous start.

## Steps

1. Capability tests:
   - `m16-map-edge-silent`: in a 32×32 map world, `nudge` toward the edge
     returns no `too_deep` and leaves no label.
   - `m16-edge-is-not-shore`: 32×32 map world with no water → `shoreTiles`
     empty, `isWetSubsoil` false on the perimeter, `isShallow` false out of
     range. With a river touching the west edge: no east-edge tile is in
     `freshShore`. Classic island: seeds 1–3 checkpoint unchanged (use the
     `switches-off` fingerprint).
   - `m16-scout-roundtrip` and `m16-scout-identity`: scout east and back west;
     after every travel step `currentIdentity` is the comarca the player is
     in; the trees list the living; going back does not say "under the sea"
     when the origin is land.
2. Add `World.outsideIsSea: boolean` — true for the classic island, false for
   map worlds (set where the world is built; find the branch that knows
   whether a geography/map is in use).
3. Add a private helper `waterOutside(x, y)` = `!inBounds(x, y) && outsideIsSea`.
   In `isShore`, `hasWaterOfKind`, `isShallow` and the movement check, use it
   for out-of-range neighbours instead of `isWater`. In `hasWaterOfKind`,
   check `inBounds` **before** indexing.
4. Leave `biomeAt` as it is (callers that draw or label the outside keep
   working); write a comment there pointing to `outsideIsSea`.
5. N36: follow the scout path in `WorldState` with the test; fix the branch
   that reinstalls the wrong start. Make the family/tribe views read the
   player's band after a comarca change (find where they pick the band).
6. Re-pin; confirm the classic seeds did **not** change (only map worlds).

## Done when

All tests pass; classic seeds 1–3 identical; map worlds re-pinned.
