## Goal

Start tests from a world that has already lived (past its first winter, with
shelters, crafts, two bands, a chiefdom) instead of simulating years each time.

## Files

New `tools/fixtures.ts`; `package.json` (script `fixtures:build`);
`.gitignore`; read only: `Simulation.fromCheckpointRecord` (~l. 922 of
`src/sim/core/Simulation.ts`), `toCheckpointRecord` (~l. 187 of
`src/sim/persistence/CheckpointRecords.ts`).

## Steps

1. `tools/fixtures.ts` exports a list of recipes:
   `{ name, scenario, seed, steps, minAlive, description }`.
2. `npm run fixtures:build` runs each recipe. If fewer than `minAlive` people
   are alive at the end, **fail with a clear message** naming the recipe;
   never save a dead world.
3. Save each to `fixtures/worlds/<name>.json`. Add `fixtures/worlds/` to
   `.gitignore`: the recipe is committed, the world is not.
4. Next to each world save its **key**: a hash of the recipe, of the
   checkpoint version constant, and of the sorted list of `Config` field
   paths. A config field added later invalidates the key.
5. `loadWorld(name, overrides?)`: if the file is missing or its key differs
   from the current one, rebuild it; then `Simulation.fromCheckpointRecord`
   and apply `overrides` to `sim.config` (used to turn a switch on over a
   saved world).
6. Initial recipes (normal worlds, never `lean`):

| Name | Recipe | For |
|---|---|---|
| `after_first_winter` | `band` config, 10,800 steps (45 days), min 6 alive | foresight |
| `band_with_shelter` | `hearths` to its end, min 8 | assembly, building |
| `village_with_crafts` | `labour` to its end, min 12 | command, craft |
| `two_rival_bands` | `conflicts` to its end, min 10 | rivalry |
| `chiefdom_with_surplus` | `polity` to its end, min 12 | command, authority |

7. Test `src/sim/__tests__/fixtures.test.ts`: load the same world twice,
   advance 200 steps each, same checkpoint hash.

## Done when

The five worlds build; deleting one makes `loadWorld` rebuild it.

## Watch out

- E7 (fixed in M15, keep the rule): never advance in one block across a
  comarca arrival; step one at a time.
- If a recipe cannot reach `minAlive`, do not lower it: R0, pick another
  scenario and say so in the diary.
- Tooling only: no version bump.
