## Goal

Storing for winter is not programmed: it is a practice someone discovers from
remembering a hungry winter, and that changes how they forage and store.

## Files

`knowledge/Tech.ts`; `knowledge/Synthesis.ts` (`Ingredient`, `satisfies`,
`describeIngredient`); `ui/TechWeb.ts`; `ai/Brain.ts` (section "Store and
withdraw", hook only).

## Steps

1. New ingredient kind `{ kind: 'remembers', drive, season }`: met if the
   person's hardship for that drive and season > 0.2. Add it to `satisfies`,
   to `describeIngredient` (with Spanish), and to the tests that walk ingredients.
2. New tech `provisioning`, kind practice, no prerequisites. Routes:
   - remembers winter hunger + carrying food + it is summer or autumn;
   - saw an empty store (`store_empty`) + remembers winter hunger.
3. Effect (only for those who know it, only with the switch on):
   - `forage`, `pick`, `hunt` add `expected(hunger) × 0.5` when the person is
     not hungry (they gather more than they eat);
   - storing food stops being a fixed 0.35 and becomes
     `0.35 + expected(hunger) × 1.5`.
4. Those who do not know it behave as today. It is taught and copied through
   the existing channels; add no new channel.
5. **Switch off and the tech:** declaring a tech changes the tech web and may
   change spark draws for everyone. To keep R13, add the tech's entry to
   `TECHS` only when the switch is on **if** the web is built from config;
   otherwise accept that the web changes, re-pin once and state it. Decide by
   reading how `TECHS` is consumed; write the choice in the diary.

## Tests

`techs-have-effects` and `spark-ingredients-are-real` pass; in the same
hand-built world, someone who knows the practice and remembers hunger stores
more than someone who does not.

## Done when

Tests pass. Note in the diary: with 15c spoilage off, storing has no decay
cost (D-9 baseline).
