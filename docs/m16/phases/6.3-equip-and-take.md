## Goal

Owner: when someone of the tribe makes a bundle and leaves it on the ground,
members who know what it is and have none equipped go and equip it, because it
gives them inventory room. (Those who do not know it investigate it — 6.4.)

## Files

`entities/Equipment.ts` (`SLOTS`), `core/Carry.ts` (`equipContainer`),
`social/Property.ts` (`mayTakeFromPile`), `ui/Hud.ts` (equipment section),
new `ai/Equip.ts` (hook in `Brain`, R14).

## Steps

1. Rule: an item with an equipment slot works only when equipped. Go through
   containers, garments and weapons and note which already obey it.
2. NPC: on taking or making an item with a free slot, equip it (same order
   path the player uses after 2.16).
3. NPC who knows the item's tech, lacks one, and sees one loose: takes it if
   `mayTakeFromPile` allows; implement D-8 in `mayTakeFromPile` if it does
   not already hold (common ground yes; inside another household's building no).
4. Score in `ai/Equip.ts`: value of the slot gain (carry room, warmth,
   protection) × knows the tech; distance through the item hash (R5).

## Tests

`m16-npc-equips-bundle`: someone makes a bundle and drops it; another of the
band who knows bundles and has none equips it; one of another band does not;
one who does not know bundles does not.

## Done when

Test passes; counter `npc_equipped_found_item`; re-pinned.
