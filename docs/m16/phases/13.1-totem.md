## Goal

Owner: a building unlocked in the tech tree that acts as the tribe's meeting
point and centre, to which NPCs return after going far; moving it moves the
tribe's centre, so a tribe can relocate by building elsewhere and moving it.

## Files

`entities/Building.ts` (append `totem`), `knowledge/Tech.ts` (append the tech),
`BandSystem.considerRelocation`, `ai/Drives.ts` (home pressure),
`art/src/` (sprite).

## Steps

1. Tech (e.g. `totem` / "Carving a totem" — pick a historically plausible
   prerequisite such as wood carving or symbolic marking; write the reason
   with `firstKnown`), with a spark route and an effect in the same pass
   (the building).
2. Building `totem`: 1×1, sticks/wood per its tech era, `places` none, no
   shelter. One per band.
3. If a band has a totem, `band.homeX/homeY` = the totem's position; home
   pressure (`Drives.ts`) points to it.
4. Moving it: demolish (14.1) and build elsewhere, or a "move the totem"
   order for the chief, with a notice to the band (R6) and a chronicle line.
5. Art generator; `art:build`; `art:sheet`.

## Tests

Without a totem nothing changes; with one, people who go far come back toward
it; moving it moves the band centre; `techs-have-effects` passes.

## Done when

Tests pass; screenshot; re-pinned.
