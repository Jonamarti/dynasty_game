## Goal

Show, with mechanism counters, what the ladder did. There is no switch, so the
comparison is the build of 2.G (baseline-2) against this one, same seeds.

## Steps

1. Cohorts, one at a time: `band` and `century`, 20 seeds, on the 2.G commit
   and on this one (check out the 2.G commit in a worktree for the "before"
   arm; never junction its `node_modules` — `AGENTS.md`).
2. Report per seed:

| Counter | Expectation (not a pass/fail gate; the owner decides) |
|---|---|
| share of adults under a roof at midnight | higher |
| `conception_no_roof` / successful rolls | lower |
| cold deaths in the first winter | not higher |
| shelters per household at end of year 1 and 2 | bounded (no growth without use) |
| lean-tos, mud huts, caves used | each > 0 somewhere |
| `building_ruined`, `ruin_removed`, `building_repaired` | > 0 |
| births per year | report |

3. Full level; screenshot tour into `artifacts/screenshots/m16-<date>-stage3/`.
4. Write the table into `docs/m16/diary.md` and a summary in the changelog.

## Done when

The table exists; anything that went the wrong way is in `docs/bugs.md` and
flagged for the owner. **Do not tune** constants here (R16).
