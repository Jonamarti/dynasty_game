## Steps

1. Inspect the generic codecs (`GraphRecords.ts`, `WorldObjectRecords.ts`,
   `CheckpointRecords.ts`, `WorldStateRecords.ts`, `TileLedger.ts`,
   `SaveFile.ts`): do not assume a new field is validated or migrated because
   the graph serialises it.
2. Save room temperature, last tick, model/config version and climate identity.
3. Validate finiteness, parameter ranges, safe tick, date consistency; reject
   future or incompatible models with a clear error.
4. Old saves stay in legacy mode. Converting them (optional) initialises rooms
   at outdoor temperature at the saved date; never claim to reconstruct history.
5. Connect the shared thermal snapshot to detailed, compact
   (`CompactBody.advance` receives a dated reading; it does not advance rooms)
   and parked comarcas (`ComarcaOffmapRuntime`, `ComarcaEcology.advanceTo`'s
   `onTick`) — one authority per comarca, shared between bands. Rates still
   legacy by default.
6. Abandoned comarca: zero occupancy, dated cooling. Transfer: temperature and
   date travel with the objects. Travellers use the route's outdoor value.
   No large-`dt` shortcuts: tick by tick first.
