## Goal

(a) The first night the ground changes colour and the second it returns — it
must not. (b) "In some games, after the second day a lot of water appears,
replacing land with a river and trapping people and animals" — never without
a cause (a dug trench, a well, rain from 15.1).

## Background

No seed reproduces it yet. Water appearing **days later** points at the 26d
refill (trenches, silos, moats filling from the shore: `World.updateShore`,
`World.FLOOD_LIMIT`) or at reinstalling a comarca after travel (M15 phase 34),
not at the river generator.

## Steps

1. Test `m16-no-sudden-flood`: run 5 days on fixed worlds (classic seeds 1–3,
   two map seeds, and any seed the owner gives); record every tile that changes
   from land to water with its **cause** (wrap the code paths that convert
   tiles to record a cause). Assert none has an unknown cause.
2. If no seed reproduces it: leave the test in place (it guards), add a
   `docs/m16/doubts.md` line asking the owner for a seed and whether in those
   games someone dug or travelled. Do not guess a fix.
3. (a) Colour: look at the night layer in `Renderer.ts`; screenshot day 1
   night, day 2 night; make the night tint independent of day count.

## Done when

Test in place and passing; (a) fixed with screenshots; (b) fixed or documented
as unreproduced in `bugs.md`.
