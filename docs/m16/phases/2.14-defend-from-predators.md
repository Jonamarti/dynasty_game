## Goal

Whoever sees a predator bite a member of their band answers as they would a
human aggressor (help, fight, chase off), subject to the same courage/need rules.

## Files

`src/sim/social/Defence.ts` (help to someone attacked; `assailantOf`),
`src/sim/systems/WildlifeSystem.ts`, `src/sim/core/AnimalAttack.ts` (find the
bite event).

## Steps

1. Test: a wolf bites a child with an adult of the band in sight → the adult
   scores a defensive action against the wolf and moves toward it.
2. Make the bite register as an assault that `Defence` can see (reuse the
   structure used for human attacks; do not write a second defence path).
3. Counter `defended_from_predator`.

## Done when

Test passes; counter appears in `band`/`wilds`; re-pinned.

## Watch out

Measure with the counter, not survival; predators cost ~11 survival points
already (C5) — this is not a balance phase.
