## Files

`social/Authority.ts` (`standingOver`), `social/Events.ts`,
`BandSystem.directWork`, `proposalCooldowns`.

## Steps

1. Only the chief. Using authority: every attendee who said no and over whom
   `standingOver(chief, listener) ≥ 0.5` accepts as **coerced**.
2. Cost per coerced: their opinion of the chief −6; event `coerced` (add to
   `Events.ts` with words and Spanish); their work on that site at 70 %.
3. Strain: `band.authorityStrain` +1 per coerced, −0.2 per day (new saved
   field, R11). Reach threshold becomes `0.5 + strain × 0.02`.
4. Refusal memory (B9): the chief does not propose the same thing to someone
   who said no for 5 days. Check whether `proposalCooldowns` already does it.
5. Allocation (B10): `directWork` picks first those who said yes; then those
   with no job or a related job; a coerced person with another job last.
6. An NPC chief uses authority if hands are short and the expected need > 0.5;
   the threshold falls with aggressiveness and attachment to tradition.

## Tests

Coercing three lowers three opinions and raises strain; with high strain
fewer people are reached.

Tests pass; R13 holds.
