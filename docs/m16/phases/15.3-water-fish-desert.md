## Background

M15 (changelog 2026-10-15 "limitar y agrupar los bancos de peces de costa",
`docs/m15_fish_cohort_20261016.md`) already clusters coastal fish around
centres with a cap of 1.5× the measured quota, using a derived stream per map;
the classic island keeps its historical loop. The cohort found no economic
reason to change the distribution further. So step 1 below is about
**proportionality to water size**, not clustering.

## Steps

1. Fish banks proportional to water: channel width and sea fraction of the
   comarca, instead of a flat cap that saturates any comarca with water.
   A creek gives fewer banks than a wide river or a coast.
2. Shellfish gathering: by hand on shallow shore tiles (crab, shellfish), no
   tool. New item(s) with nutrition and a source (15.2 tiles), effect in the
   same pass.
3. Real fishing wants concentration spots and a new tech, **rod**, with its
   effect in the same pass. Check how `fishing`, net and `fish_trap` fit;
   write the resulting table in the diary.
4. `desert` biome on the local map with its habitat (few herds, no berries) and
   water from the ground (`well` exists) and from plants.
5. Tundra harder through habitat (fewer plants/herds), not a multiplier.

## Tests

A creek yields fewer banks than a wide river and a coast; a local desert has
no 17 herds; shellfish needs no tool; fishing a concentration spot needs the
rod; `techs-have-effects` passes.
