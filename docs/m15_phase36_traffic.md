# M15 phase 36 — root traffic coordinator

`WorldTrafficCoordinator` is the owner adapter between `WorldState`, `WorldCaravans`, and persisted party checkpoints. `dispatchCaravan()` accepts a living, non-player local NPC who knows `trade`, a known all-land route with physical transport, and a caller-selected cargo offer already present in the merchant's inventory. It stages the source and transit simulations against a cloned shared `IdSpace`, removes only the offered stack from the merchant, and publishes both owners after checkpoint validation. `dispatchParty()` is the common canonical owner transfer for raids and routes selected from an explicit parked source.

`advance(state, tick)` advances only the ticket's listed people through contiguous `advanceJourneyTick` calls. It never calls the full detailed `step()` on a second motor. A due arrival carries its original event and party IDs in the returned `TrafficArrival`; the service transfers those exact Person objects to the existing active or parked destination, or creates the destination comarca once, then credits the physically escrowed goods to the merchant. After arrival the party checkpoint becomes `null`: the active/parked comarca owns the people and the traffic record retains route/return metadata without a second copy.

`orderCampTrade()` uses the active destination's people spatial hash and the existing `trade` order. `processCamps(state, tick)` waits one in-game day after arrival, lets an active detailed trade finish, or in a parked destination barters one physically held non-food item against actual nearby inventory at no greater `ItemDef.baseValue`; it emits the normal trade social event only after both inventory legs succeed. It then escrows only non-food goods held by the merchant (travel provisions stay available to `advanceJourneyTick`) and schedules the return route. A dead merchant closes the schedule as `cancelled`, leaving the canonical corpse and other travellers at the destination. Macro-only inventories cannot be bartered because no named physical stock is owned there. `resolveRaid()` hands the pending raid event and actual transit Simulation to the root combat resolver, then acknowledges only after the root applies the result. Band relation lookups use real local band IDs (zero is a valid ID); macro people map to their root-generated band IDs at the coordinator boundary. A new destination first materializes its actual root-owned macro residents through `prepareTrafficResidents()` before the caravan party transfers in.

`validateOwners()` checks the active roster, parked active rosters, a pending ordinary journey, and every in-flight traffic checkpoint for duplicate living owners. The versioned `WorldTrafficRecord` contains scheduler state and one party checkpoint per in-flight route. The root owns the v5 `WorldStateRecord` traffic field and validates scheduler plus checkpoint ownership on restore. A first visit to a destination uses `prepareTrafficResidents()` to withdraw real macro residents before placing the party; inventories remain attached to those actual people, and the service never mints an abstract stockpile. The trade conservation test seeds a physical `tin_ore` stack in a counterpart inventory to test barter accounting only; it does not claim the world currently produces tin ore.

Focused root tests construct a real geographic `WorldState`, dispatch a named NPC merchant with actual inventory, round-trip the traffic record mid-route, and assert the person has exactly one owner at arrival. They also verify that an unavailable offer leaves the source checkpoint byte-identical.
