# M15 phase 36d: raids across comarcas

Raids start from an existing grievance between peoples. A band still needs its ordinary instigator, chief approval, war party and quorum. If no victim structure is locally reachable, the instigator may propose a remote route only to a comarca where that person has already met the victim people and which the world geography says is land. A route never reads another comarca's hidden population or stores.

The world root queues the accepted party during the simulation step and transfers the named people after the step. On arrival, the party uses the destination's real people and buildings: a visible store can be taken, a visible structure can be sabotaged, nearby people can be attacked, and an empty edge sends the party toward the victim band's camp. Capture remains the existing local, witnessed behavior.

Caravan raid notices are resolved against an actual hostile person near the convoy at its destination. The attacker receives only cargo their physical carrying capacity accepts. The destination records a witnessed theft; the merchant receives the same event as its firsthand victim memory, with its comarca origin, so it can carry that story home. If no actual hostile person is there, the notice is acknowledged without inventing attackers or consuming cargo.

Focused coverage lives in `src/sim/__tests__/world-raids.test.ts` and the cross-comarca BandSystem case in `src/sim/__tests__/band.test.ts`. No UI changed in this phase.
